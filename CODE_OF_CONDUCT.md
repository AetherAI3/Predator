# Predator community guidelines

Help make the public research and training records useful to beginners and experienced readers alike.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique claims and methods with specific evidence.
- Keep questions, corrections, and suggestions relevant to Predator's public work.
- Clearly separate observed results, hypotheses, and opinions.
- Respect privacy and authorized scope. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive reports.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, spam, and promotional flooding.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that disrupts the community or exposes private information. Report a conduct concern through [Aether's contact channel](https://aethersystems.net/contact?intent=general&product=site_wide&cta=footer_updates_contact), with links to the relevant public context where appropriate.
